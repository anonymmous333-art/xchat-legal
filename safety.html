XChat Child Safety Standards

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Zero tolerance: XChat prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, and any sexualization of children.

1. Scope and age safety

XChat is a social communication service. Users must meet the minimum age required by applicable law and the age requirement shown during registration. We use age-related safety controls and may restrict, suspend, or remove accounts that present a safety risk to minors.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

Users may not create, upload, request, share, store, livestream, or distribute CSAM or any content that sexually exploits, endangers, grooms, or sexualizes a child. Attempts to arrange sexual contact with a minor, solicit sexual images from a minor, or direct a minor to another service for exploitation are prohibited.

3. Reporting suspected child exploitation

Users should report suspected CSAE or CSAM immediately through XChat's in-app reporting tools. Include the username, profile or message link when available, a short description, and any relevant time or channel information. Do not download, forward, or otherwise redistribute suspected CSAM.

For urgent danger, contact local emergency services. XChat may preserve relevant information, restrict accounts, remove content, and report suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities or recognized reporting organizations where required or appropriate.

4. Moderation and enforcement

XChat uses user reports, automated safety signals, human review, access controls, and account actions to prevent and respond to abuse. We may remove content, disable features, suspend or permanently terminate accounts, block users, preserve evidence, and cooperate with lawful requests. We do not guarantee that every harmful item will be detected immediately, so reporting remains important.

5. Appeals and safety contact

Users may appeal an enforcement action by contacting us with the affected username, action date, and explanation. Appeals do not restore prohibited content while a child-safety review is pending.
