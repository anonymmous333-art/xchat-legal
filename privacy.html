XChat Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XChat collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the XChat mobile application and related services.

1. Information we collect

XChat may collect your email address, username, name, profile information, photos, videos, messages, comments, reactions, reports, blocked-user settings, approximate location, and device notification information.

2. Camera, microphone and media

XChat requests camera and microphone access when you use camera, video, live, call, or voice-note features. Photos and videos that you choose to upload may be stored in secure cloud storage and shown according to your privacy settings.

3. Location

XChat may use approximate location for map and nearby features. XChat is designed not to publish your exact address publicly.

4. How we use information

We use information to provide account access, messaging, profiles, stories, live video, maps, notifications, moderation, security, and social features.

5. Service providers

XChat uses Firebase for authentication, database, and media storage. XChat may use Agora for real-time voice, video, or live features.

6. Sharing and visibility

Public posts and profiles may be visible to other users. Private content is shown according to the selected privacy settings. XChat does not sell personal information.

7. Disappearing content

Some messages, photos, videos, and stories may expire or become unavailable after a configured time or number of views. No online service can guarantee that a recipient has not captured content with another device.

8. Security

XChat uses encrypted network connections and access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your XChat account and associated personal data by emailing anonymmous333@gmail.com.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete your personal information. Contact us for privacy requests.

11. Children

XChat is not directed to children below the minimum age required by applicable law.
